# Manual test script

For a person, or an agent driving a browser. Run `deno task dev` and open the
local URL with `?seed=121`. Before each section, reload. To read logical state,
use `game.state` in the console. To read what has played, use the panel beside
the grid, including the `#live` text, or look at the marker.

**Promotion rule:** a bug found by hand or by an agent becomes a permanent test
in `core/` before it is fixed, unless it truly lives in `game/`, in which case
add a step here.

| #  | Action                                                                                                                              | Expected observation                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Load the page                                                                                                                       | One canvas. A pale marker at the center of a map of green beach, dark green forest, blue dungeon and gray ocean cells. No console errors.                                             |
| 2  | Tap the right arrow                                                                                                                 | The marker slides one cell east. `#live` says `moved to 1, 0`. `player` is `{ x: 1, y: 0 }`.                                                                                          |
| 3  | Walk until ocean is ahead, then press toward it                                                                                     | The marker lurches and flashes red, then returns to its cell. `#live` says `blocked by ocean at ...`. `player` has not changed.                                                       |
| 4  | Tap an arrow during a walk                                                                                                          | The tap waits. The marker finishes the walk, then takes that step. It never skips a cell.                                                                                             |
| 5  | Mash arrows quickly, then stop                                                                                                      | The marker settles on the cell `player` reports.                                                                                                                                      |
| 6  | Hold an arrow down                                                                                                                  | The marker keeps walking. Holding into a wall repeats the bump.                                                                                                                       |
| 7  | Load `?seed=7`                                                                                                                      | A different map.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 8  | Run `game.state` in the console                                                                                                     | Plain data: `seed`, `player`, `inventory`, `changed` and `won`. No terrain list. `changed` lists only the cells you gathered from, not the whole map. `won` is `false`.               |
| 9  | Press an arrow several times                                                                                                        | Position and Terrain in the panel change as each step plays, and never ahead of the marker.                                                                                           |
| 10 | Make the window short, then press the down arrow                                                                                    | The page does not scroll. The marker moves.                                                                                                                                           |
| 11 | Click the sound button, then press an arrow                                                                                         | The button reads `Sound: off`, and the arrow still moves the marker.                                                                                                                  |
| 12 | Click the sound button again                                                                                                        | The button reads `Sound: on`, and you hear a two-note chime.                                                                                                                          |
| 13 | Tab to the sound button and press Space                                                                                             | It toggles. Arrow keys still move the marker.                                                                                                                                         |
| 14 | Listen while walking, bumping and gathering (headphones or speakers)                                                                | A soft tick on each step, a low buzz on each bump, and a tone on each gather. **This is the only check that a person can hear them.**                                                 |
| 15 | On a high-resolution display, or at browser zoom 200%                                                                               | Grid edges stay sharp. The marker stays centered. Run `document.querySelector("canvas").width`: it is the canvas `clientWidth` times `devicePixelRatio`.                              |
| 16 | On a beach cell, press `G`                                                                                                          | `#live` says `Gathered ...`. The cell turns darker green. Terrain reads `beach (spent)`; stepping off shows plain `beach`.                                                            |
| 17 | Walk about 20 cells away, back, then gather there again                                                                             | The cell is still dark and Terrain reads `beach (spent)`. Nothing is added: the count in `#live` does not go up.                                                                      |
| 18 | On an ungathered cell, press `G`                                                                                                    | A pale square pops outward over the cell and fades.                                                                                                                                   |
| 19 | Press `G` on your first cell                                                                                                        | A line such as `stone: 1` appears in the inventory list, at the same moment the pop starts and not before.                                                                            |
| 20 | Gather on forest after beach                                                                                                        | A second line appears in the list, and both counts are correct.                                                                                                                       |
| 21 | Press `G` on a spent cell                                                                                                           | No pop, no sound, no change to the list, and `#live` is unchanged.                                                                                                                    |
| 22 | Hold `G` down                                                                                                                       | Exactly one gather, not repeats.                                                                                                                                                      |
| 23 | Tap an arrow, then `G` before the marker arrives                                                                                    | The walk finishes, then the gather happens on the new cell. Tapping another arrow and then `G` quickly during a walk lets the `G` replace that arrow: the latest tap wins, by design. |
| 24 | Mash arrows and `G`, then stop                                                                                                      | The marker is on the cell in `game.state.player`, and the inventory list matches `game.state.inventory`.                                                                              |
| 25 | Developer check: set `WALK_MS` to 3000, walk onto a gatherable cell and press `G` during the walk                                   | The inventory list and Terrain stay unchanged until the walk finishes and the pop starts. Undo the `WALK_MS` change afterward.                                                        |
| 26 | Load the page with an empty inventory                                                                                               | The recipe list shows each recipe with what it needs, such as `Make campfire (needs 2 wood, 1 stone)`.                                                                                |
| 27 | Gather the inputs for a recipe, then click its button                                                                               | A pop plays on the player's cell, a two-note craft tone sounds, `#live` says `Made campfire from 2 wood, 1 stone`, and the inventory list updates. The button relabels to `Make ...`. |
| 28 | Click a recipe button while short of inputs                                                                                         | A low deny tone sounds, `#live` says `Cannot make ...: need ...`, and the inventory list does not change.                                                                             |
| 29 | Set `WALK_MS` to 3000, start a walk, then click a ready recipe                                                                      | Nothing shows until the walk finishes; the craft then plays, and only then does the button relabel. Undo the `WALK_MS` change afterward.                                              |
| 30 | Click a recipe button with the mouse, then press an arrow                                                                           | The arrow moves the marker: the click gave the keys back to the game.                                                                                                                 |
| 31 | Tab to a recipe button and press Enter                                                                                              | It crafts (or reports why not), and focus stays on the button, so Tab and Enter still work for the next recipe.                                                                       |
| 32 | Craft a magic catalyst, walk into the dungeon core, and press `G`                                                                   | `#live` says `Gathered core shard (you have 1)`. The cell turns darker purple.                                                                                                        |
| 33 | Gather 3 core shards, 2 mana crystals and 1 torch, then click `Make heart of the dungeon`                                           | A pop plays, a rising four-note chord sounds, `#live` says `The heart of the dungeon is forged. ... You have won.`, and the win banner fades in over the page.                        |
| 34 | After the win, press arrows, press `G`, and click recipe buttons                                                                    | Nothing moves, nothing plays: every command is refused. `game.state.won` is `true`, and `game.state` has not changed.                                                                 |
| 35 | With exactly one lockpick left and the other required ingredients, attempt `Make magic catalyst` while standing in dungeon terrain. | The deny tone should play, #live explains the craft was refused, and the inventory list should not change. The arrows still move the marker inside the dungeon.                       |
